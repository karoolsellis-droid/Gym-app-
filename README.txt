ANDRE HEALTH — phone app (installable web app)

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop on a computer (free account).
2. Drag this whole "andre-health-app" folder onto the page. Netlify gives you a link like https://something.netlify.app
3. Open that link on your phone:
   - Android (Chrome): tap "Install" on the green banner.
   - iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
4. Open the app from your home screen, tap "Load starter or backup file" and choose andre-health-starter.json
   (keep that file private — it is NOT inside this folder on purpose).

Your data stays on your phone. Use Settings > Back up now and then.
